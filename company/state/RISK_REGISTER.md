"Trigger" = observable signal that the risk is materialising. "Check" = the date or gate at which the owner shows the mitigation's evidence and Assurance re-scores. Review at every gate.
Risks are never deleted. A closed risk keeps its row with Status `Closed (<date>): <reason>`.
