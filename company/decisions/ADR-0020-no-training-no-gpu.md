# ADR-0020: No custom model training or GPU infra until data shows need
- **Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Decider:** Founder (CFO/CTO memo + competitor scan) · **Door:** two-way · **Review:** M1 gate
- **Process note:** Founder decision recorded directly; no position vote was held.

**Decision:** No custom model training, fine-tuning or GPU infrastructure until measured data shows a need that recipes, routing and caching cannot meet. Consistent with CLAUDE.md rule 6 and ADR-0013.
