# ADR-0007: Product = Creative Director; edit-as-code is the hidden engine
- **Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Decider:** Founder (after 3 independent stakeholder strategy memos) · **Door:** one-way-ish · **Review:** M1 gate
- **Process note:** Founder decision recorded directly; no T3 five-position vote was held. Positions may file dissent at the M1 gate.

**Decision:** The product is an AI Creative Director. Edit-as-code (timeline.json + ops) is the hidden engine, never the user-facing concept. Primary UI = one-tap preview cards (2 variants by default in alpha); chat is the power/fallback layer. Supersedes the chat-first framing of PR/FAQ v0 (H-0021).
