## Risk opinion on Engineering Open question 1 (Python floor). The Founder decides; this is advice only.
- **Option (a), raise the floor to `>=3.12` and run CI on 3.12 (better: a 3.12 + 3.14 matrix).** Risk is low. It makes `requires-python` true (closes F1), and it keeps the exact versions with which E6 and the existing ingest/render evidence were produced. It also lets CI cover the MILP and ASR deps that Phase 1 needs (closes most of F2). Cost: users on 3.11 are excluded. The product runs locally in a Codespace that already has 3.12 and 3.14, so this cost is close to zero.
- **Option (b), stay on 3.11 and downgrade.** Risk is medium. It needs at least 5 downgrades, not 2 (`PuLP` 3.3.2, `numpy` ≤ 2.4, `av` ≤ 18.1.1, and PuLP's transitive `orloge` and `cpsat-logutils` disappear or change). `av` is the decoder in the ingest path, and the CLAUDE.md gotcha already shows PyAV version coupling to be fragile. Existing evidence produced on 3.14 (E6, ingest) would need to be rerun to remain valid under I-1. Gain: 3.11 compatibility that no current user needs.
- **Do nothing (status quo).** Not acceptable past M0. The metadata claims a floor that the runtime cannot meet, and CI would stay green while `select` breaks.
- **Assurance's view:** (a), with a CI matrix that includes the `.venv` minor version. Whichever option is chosen, F1 must be closed (metadata true, comments complete) before the M0 gate.
