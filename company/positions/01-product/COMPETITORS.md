## AdKraft (added 2026-11-23, H-0031 addendum)
- **What it does:** Shopify app (developer: Adkraft AI, Dubai) that makes short AI video ads from a product name, description and photos: writes, storyboards, renders and voices, and delivers an MP4 for Reels/TikTok/Shorts. Product-ad generator, not a footage editor. Arabic/RTL support: not stated on the listing (unverified).
- **Pricing model:** credits. Free install with 500 credits (ads up to 15 s); Starter $45/month for 1,200 credits (about 16 fast 15-s ads); Pro $99/month for 2,800 credits (20-30 s ads); optional Fast Ad Packs billed by Shopify: 5 ads $15, 10 ads $30, 25 ads $75.
- **The ~$3/video figure:** verified from the primary listing as the ad-pack rate ($15 / 5 ads = $3.00 per ad; $75 / 25 = $3.00). Subscription math gives about $2.80 per ad ($45 / 16, derived by us, not stated). Source: https://apps.shopify.com/adkraft, fetched 2026-11-23 (via an automated page summariser, so E1; Founder should eyeball the page once). The developer site https://adkraft.ai/pricing returned no pricing content on 2026-11-23.
- **Our differentiator (hypothesis, E0):** we edit the user's own footage into Arabic-captioned Shorts (edit-as-code, local render, near-zero marginal cost per UNIT_ECONOMICS) rather than generating ads from product photos. Serves P2 and P3 with real footage. We use $3/video only as a reference point in the pricing fake door; we do not copy it.
